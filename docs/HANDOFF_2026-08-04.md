## What is implemented

- Seven-day, Google-account-bound Firestore trial with restore after reinstall.
- Profile shows `Sign in with Google` after logout. Selecting an account reads
  its existing trial without silently starting a new one: active time restores,
  an expired trial stays expired, and an unused account remains pre-trial.
- Google Play non-consumable lifetime unlock, purchase restore, receipt
  verification and product-detail retry/recovery. Restore directly queries
  Play-owned products, so a successful empty result clears a locally cached
  purchase after refund/revocation; a Play/network error retains offline grace.
  Returning from Play refreshes ownership automatically, and the paywall closes
  on confirmed ownership even when an active trial already grants access.
- Light theme by default; user-selectable light/dark theme and money font.
- User-selectable kilometres or miles and CAD or USD labels. Selecting USD
  defaults distance to miles; CAD defaults to kilometres, and Distance remains
  manually overridable. Offer distances remain stored canonically in
  kilometres. U.S. `mi`, `mile`, and `miles` offer-card formats convert during
  parsing. Currency selection never performs foreign-exchange conversion.
- Uber, Lyft and Hopp strict offer parsing, including Lyft bonuses, Lyft
  add-to-queue offers, multi-stop totals, and fail-safe rejection of incomplete
  cards.
- Hopp accepted-trip inference backed by the 2026-08-04 device flow: Arrived,
  timer-shaped Waiting, Start Trip, End Trip, Confirm Price and Rate passenger.
- Read-only accepted-trip inference for the supplied Uber/Lyft trip flows.
  Ambiguous transitions remain Unconfirmed rather than being guessed.
- Redesigned History outcome cards/details, bonus/queue fields, session stats,
  CSV export, collapsed-by-default filters with one global All reset, and a
  themed 2×2 summary. The collapsed row names every active filter and fare
  floor in one truncated-safe line, with an amber spotlight on its icon. Accepted is
  counted separately as a subset of the filtered total; it is never added to
  the Good/OK/Bad verdict partition. Accepted mode includes only strong
  confirmed `taken` outcomes; Unconfirmed is never folded into Accepted.
- Outcome inference targets the newest unresolved offer from the matching
  platform, so a Hopp acceptance cannot mark a newer Uber/Lyft offer Taken.
  Cross-app accessibility events also cannot clear or extend another app's
  verdict pill, and frame deduplication is isolated per package.
- The verdict pill intentionally uses a compact `$` label for both CAD and USD;
  selected distance units still render as `km` or `mi`.
- The trial card shows hours/minutes on the final day and refreshes once per
  minute.
- Samsung overlay gray-window race fix, hero spacing correction and shared
  plasma pulse around the live hero/pill.
- Updated Fox Tips copy supplied on 2026-08-04.
- Play Console-ready 512×512 launcher artwork at
  `assets/branding/foxyco_launcher_playstore_512.png`.

## Trial failure: root cause and confirmed fix

Play builds repeatedly failed after account selection with
`google-sign-in/sign_in_failed`. This was not a connectivity bug and was not
caused by having multiple Google accounts on the phone.

The Firebase Android OAuth client had the wrong Play App Signing SHA-1. The
actual signer was extracted from the Play-delivered APK (`13.apk`):

```text
SHA-1   CC:8B:F7:D5:53:81:CF:57:70:98:38:48:13:5A:2E:08:56:4C:88:DC
SHA-256 1C:04:D1:6E:BE:08:FD:91:3D:8B:03:87:A0:FF:94:10:CF:D1:18:C6:AB:7B:8A:EA:30:7A:10:C7:EA:28:91:53
```

The previously entered SHA-1 beginning `5C:38:29:2F` was not the certificate
signing the installed APK. After adding the actual SHA-1 in Firebase,
downloading a fresh `google-services.json`, and installing the new Play build,
the same account successfully restored its trial with two days remaining.

`android/app/google-services.json`, upload/debug signing material,
`key.properties`, passwords and keystores are deliberately ignored and must
never be committed. A new machine needs the current Firebase file locally.

## Final audit result

Automated checks cover parser fixtures, scoring, settings migration,
cross-isolate payloads, billing/trial state, History and UI behavior. The app:

- passes all 309 Flutter tests and `flutter analyze` with no issues;
- produces a release AAB containing the Play licensing public key, byte-for-byte
  matching the named `dist/` copy;

- requests no location permission;
- includes no ads, analytics or crash-reporting SDK;
- keeps offer content/history on the phone;
- uses network access only for trial auth/state and Play purchase validation;
- cannot perform accessibility gestures and never auto-accepts;
- has backup disabled, release shrinking enabled and secrets ignored;
- fails closed on ambiguous parsing and missing entitlement.

Historical note: the build now truthfully uses
`android:isAccessibilityTool="false"` because FoxyCo is not a disability aid.
Android may therefore hide `accessibilityDataSensitive` gig-card views, so
do not change the flag to bypass a compatibility failure. The Play
declaration and consent video must describe the behavior honestly: read-only
offer analysis, narrowly scoped to the three driver apps, no automation.

Google Play may publicly show a monetized developer's verified legal identity
and address. That comes from the Play/merchant profile, not FoxyCo code. Do not
promise that it can be hidden where Google requires disclosure; investigate a
compliant business or mailbox address separately if personal-address privacy
is a concern.
