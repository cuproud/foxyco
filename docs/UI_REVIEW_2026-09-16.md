## Overall assessment

FoxyCo has a coherent, recognizable identity and substantially more working
depth than a simple offer calculator. The four tabs, live-control card, Rules
preview, editable History, and appearance controls fit together. The fox/car
assets and small car-shaped slider handles are already distinctive. The next
step is greater clarity and reliability, not more decoration or more tabs.

The strongest features to demonstrate to potential testers are the real
offer verdict and the Rules preview. Settings breadth is useful after someone
understands that core benefit, not as the opening marketing message.

## Feature priority

1. **Reliable overlay and offer detection.** Investigate the exact Lyft →
   external Maps handoff and stacked Radar misses before adding visual effects.
   Build 108 adds diagnostics, not a proven fix.
2. **Cancellation earnings.** Current details allow payout editing for accepted,
   completed, or already-edited records, not a newly cancelled record. Add a
   fee received amount (including zero) while retaining Cancelled status and
   the original quote. Include that fee in received earnings, but never count
   the untravelled quoted route as completed distance or use quoted trip time
   for cancellation hourly rates. Actual time/distance can remain optional.
3. **Earnings confidence.** Separate estimated accepted value from confirmed
   received money, with a count of records needing reconciliation. Do not
   imply an estimated total is money already paid.
4. **Outcome provenance.** Show inferred versus manually corrected status;
   manual corrections should remain authoritative. Do not promote diagnostic
   route-shadow matches into automatic acceptance without validated evidence.
5. **First successful offer.** Make the existing preview easy to discover and
   clearly simulated. Guide a new tester through permissions, watched apps,
   rules and one successful real offer before introducing all customization.

## Motion: enhance what is already distinctive

- Slide-to-live: one brief fox/car ignition or highlight sweep when activation
  succeeds, then settle. No repeated celebration while waiting for offers.
- Rules: let the existing car handle move with input and settle gently; update
  preview verdict color once when its category actually changes.
- History: a brief saved-state check after a manual correction. Do not animate
  every amount from zero whenever opening the tab.
- Offer pill: keep economics readable immediately. Any decorative motion must
  not delay, obscure or repeatedly bounce the numbers while driving.

Suggested durations of roughly 150–250ms are design starting points, not
benchmarked guarantees. Prefer local transform/color transitions over animated
blur, glow, shadows or repeated layout changes. Respect reduced motion and
stop decorative motion when not visible. RootShell already uses TickerMode
for inactive tabs; retain that safeguard rather than claiming it is missing.

Profile UI and raster times on the S24 Ultra and a lower-end supported phone,
including simultaneous Maps/driver-app use. Target the actual refresh-rate
budget (about 8.3ms at 120Hz, 16.7ms at 60Hz), and compare battery/thermal
behavior over a shift. No video review can promise zero performance issues.
See [Flutter performance guidance](https://docs.flutter.dev/perf/best-practices)
and [profile-mode measurement](https://docs.flutter.dev/perf/ui-performance).

## Tester recruitment

The supplied [Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/uberdriverscanada/comments/1w6rh9s/looking_for_android_gig_drivers_in_canadaus_to/)
uses a branded poster showing Home rather than a real offer being evaluated.
Its artwork has no visible enrollment steps and shows a nearly expired trial.
That can create uncertainty about the benefit and cost. The accessible page
does not establish every comment/link a visitor might see, so this is not a
claim that no signup link exists anywhere. No signup/conversion analytics were
provided; the reason for zero testers is not proven.

Test a short real offer → verdict clip, explicit Android/platform availability,
accurate privacy explanation, actual tester access terms, and one clear
enrollment instruction with clickable links. Ask for a small, specific cohort
and explain what feedback you need. Track post views, enrollment-page visits,
opt-ins, installs and first successful offer separately before concluding that
the product or design is the problem. Do not post or contact people automatically.
